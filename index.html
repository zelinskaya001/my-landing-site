Three days. Three sets.

16Friday
Set 01First serve

The doors open, the first balls fly and you finally meet your doubles partner. Ease into the weekend on the social courts, then catch the opening team matches and live music after sunset.

17Saturday
Set 02The rivalry

White versus Black, all afternoon. The matches are grouped by playing level, but every point adds to your team’s score. Between games, there’s plenty of time to watch, practise or find a new partner.

18Sunday
Set 03The final set

The last matches decide the winning side. There’s a trophy to lift, a final performance on centre court and no reason to rush home.
